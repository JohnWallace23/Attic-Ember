{%- comment -%} YAML reads `price: 30.00` as a number, so Liquid hands it back as "30.0" and every price on the site read like a measurement instead of money. Liquid has no sprintf, so rebuild the cents by hand. Usage: {% include money.html amount=page.price %} -> 30.00 (prints the number only — put the $ in the caller) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cents = include.amount | times: 100.0 | round -%} {%- assign whole = cents | divided_by: 100 -%} {%- assign part = cents | modulo: 100 -%} {%- if part < 10 -%}{%- assign part = part | prepend: "0" -%}{%- endif -%} {{- whole }}.{{ part -}}
